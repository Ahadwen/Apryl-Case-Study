01 · The problem

Not a back-office problem. Not a UX problem.
A collaboration problem.

Reviewers know what a claim needs. Members hold the documents, and sometimes only the clinic can provide them. The goal: help the member understand what's needed from them, as seamlessly as possible, before they submit.

Today
Member submits→
Reviewer finds what's missing→ Request sent to member→ Member asks clinic→ Clinic sends document→ Member re-uploads
→ Decision
Proposed
Member sees exactly what's needed→ Member gathers it, asking the clinic early→ Clinic sends document→ Member submits a complete claim→ Reviewed once→ Decision
