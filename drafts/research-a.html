02 · Research

What we know, what we assume, what we'll check

What we know

From the claims team

  • 2 FTE review claims (alongside digital card transactions); 2 more hires planned for 2026
  • Requirements are a matrix: country × treatment × employer
  • An invoice is always required; proof of payment usually
  • Proof of medical necessity drives back-and-forth between member, Apryl and clinic
  • Multi-document uploads are split by hand
  • Every claim is signed off by two people (four-eyes)

What we assume

To be tested

  • Most of the 40% of information requests are preventable at upload
  • Most claims are straightforward
  • Most of the delay is the round trips between member, Apryl and clinic, not time spent reviewing
  • A claim is created when the member opens it, and moves to "submitted" when submitted

What we'll check

Weeks 1–2

  • Sit with reviewers for a day each
  • Inventory the knowledge in people's heads: country exceptions, judgement calls, Slack escalations
  • Baseline the metrics
  • Legal and DPIA advice, started in week one
  • Proof of medical necessity: what counts, who issues it, and when it's required
