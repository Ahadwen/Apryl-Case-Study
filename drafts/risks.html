Risks and scoping

Risks

  • Legal confirmation is the critical path. No AI on real documents until it's done.
  • Needs the claims team's time: to write down rules, and for us to sit with them and understand their workflows.
  • OCR quality on bad photos.
  • Captured requirements are incomplete: claims arrive "complete" but still need a request.
  • A locked Submit button puts members off: watched through the abandonment rate.
  • Clinics may be slow to send proof of medical necessity.
  • Anonymised medical letters can still contain health details.

Scoping

In scope

  • Claim type and checklist
  • Document reading, masking and extraction
  • Member confirmation and gated submit
  • Operator view with sources
  • Metrics and dashboards

Out of scope

  • Fraud, payout, employer configuration tooling
  • Changes to how operators make judgement calls

Team: about 2 engineers, plus time from the claims team.
