03 · Proposal

One checklist, shared by member and operator

Today, only the operator knows what a claim needs. We put that knowledge at the start: each member sees exactly what their claim requires, gathers it with help, and can only submit once it's complete. Operators receive complete, confirmed claims.

What changes for the member

  • They say what they're claiming for. With their country and employer, that sets the checklist.
  • The checklist asks for a few mandatory documents, then information any document can supply. Only documents that identify the member count.
  • If proof of medical necessity is needed, they're told upfront, with a ready-made message for their clinic. They can save and come back.
  • Facts are pre-filled; they confirm each one. Missing or unreadable: upload another document, or type it in, flagged for a person to check.

What changes for the operator

  • Deterministic checks run automatically.
  • Each fact is shown with its source highlighted.
  • Two lanes: clean claims (verify and sign off) and judgement claims (AI summary, person decides).
  • Both reviewers sign off either way: the four-eyes check stays.

Privacy: documents are read and masked on the device. The AI never learns who the member is.

Machine vs. human: deterministic checks are automated, extraction is assisted, judgement stays with people. Each check earns its autonomy through measured accuracy.

Alternative considered: AI judging the whole claim. Rejected because it's harder to audit, version and defend.
