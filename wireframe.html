Open questions

  1. How do we recognise the name and DOB? Proposed: the patient is logged in, so we already know them; we look for those exact values in the text (tolerant of formats like 3 Feb 1990 / 03.02.1990), which also proves the document is theirs. Patterns catch other identifiers (NHS no., phone, email, address).
  2. Which fields? The list above is my guess at a reimbursement claim. What's the real list?
  3. One document or several? E.g. invoice + prescription together filling one list.
